Research

What I work on

Advised by Dr. Sudip Mittal

Agentic AI for Autonomous Cyber Defense

Multi-agent systems that mine threat intelligence, generate deployable detection rules and orchestrate intrusion response — with the failure modes and attack surfaces of the agent layer itself treated as a first-class problem, not an afterthought.

Knowledge Contextualization

Turning global cyber knowledge into intelligence an individual organization can act on: knowledge-graph provenance to defeat fabricated CTI, retrieval grounded in local context, and ontologies that make intrusion response machine-readable.

Adversarial AI & Explainability

Representation learning and domain adaptation that make models robust against adversarial manipulation while staying interpretable — including activation steering to suppress sycophancy and hallucination in high-stakes question answering.

Representation Learning Across Domains

Graph and multimodal representations applied where the data is heterogeneous: control-flow graphs for polymorphic malware, contrastive embeddings for malware classification, and LLM-engineered knowledge graphs for clinical and patient-journey data.
